Paid Ad Strategy

Intentional Ad Deployment

Initial campaigns focus on warming audiences. Conversion campaigns are intentionally delayed until audiences are pre-qualified through organic content and engagement.

Phase 1

Engagement Campaigns

Build interactions, follows, and shares that signal audience intent and feed the retargeting pool.

Phase 1

Video Views

Maximizes reach of educational content and creates custom audiences based on watch time behavior.

Phase 1–2

Reach & Awareness

Broad brand exposure campaigns that establish recognition at the top of the funnel.

Phase 2

Traffic Campaigns

Drive warmed audiences to landing pages, blog posts, and educational resources.

Phase 2–3

Retargeting

Serve precision ads to audiences who've already engaged — dramatically lowering cost-per-lead.

Phase 4

Lead Generation

Full conversion campaigns launched after trust is established for maximum ROI and lead quality.
